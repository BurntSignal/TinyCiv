# TinyCiv 0.5.5

- Replaced effectively unbounded exponential population growth with a dynamic, self-regulating carrying-capacity model.
- There is no hard maximum population. Capacity can continue expanding indefinitely through development, technology, trade, public works, and new settlements.
- Added hidden food, housing, sanitation, and logistics capacity so different bottlenecks can emerge independently.
- Population pressure now slows fertility and growth naturally before severe shortages occur.
- Crowding and infrastructure strain can affect food, health, morale, stability, migration, illness severity, civic friction, and event frequency.
- Sustained population pressure can produce Chronicle-worthy consequences or major responses without logging routine day-to-day friction.
- Discoveries, institutions, public works, trade, strong harvests, and new settlements can expand carrying capacity and create new growth waves.
- Very high civilization metrics are no longer permanently sticky at 100%; large societies can function extremely well without behaving like frictionless utopias.
- Existing v0.5.4 worlds are migrated conservatively so a prosperous established civilization does not suffer an artificial update-day collapse.
- Preserved all v0.5.4 behavior and fixes, including the observer-belief `{root}` repair.
